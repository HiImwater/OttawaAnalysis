# 🧠 LifeGuide AI • Jordan's Unified Personal Life OS & Spatial Cockpit

> **A single-screen, context-aware, self-evolving personal operating system built with real-time directional GPS, freehand map drawing, natural-language omni-stream parsing, and live life telemetry.**

---

## 🌟 The Core Problem Solved
Traditional apps are fragmented: Google Maps for navigation, Apple Notes for scratchpads, banking apps for cash flow, PadMapper for housing, and calendars for appointments. 

**LifeGuide AI unifies everything into a single screen:**
1. **The Map & Live Directional Location Never Disappear:** Housing pins, clinics, errands, food spots, notes, and custom drawings live together on the interactive Leaflet GIS canvas.
2. **💭 Omni-Input Stream ("Do Everything" Bar):** Type or dictate *any* real-world event (`"Spent $16 on dinner"`, `"Found room on Nelson for $820"`, `"Toe is throbbing"`, `"pin: good cafe"`), and the autonomous AI engine parses entities, calculates live balances, drops map pins, and evolves your schedule automatically.
3. **✏️ Freehand Map Drawing & Spatial Annotation:** Draw directly on the map with glowing neon ink (Cyan, Emerald, Rose, Amber), circle search zones, drop custom pins, and have all drawings persist across sessions via `localStorage`.
4. **📝 Integrated Manual Scratchpad:** Instant auto-saving markdown notepad right alongside the map with 1-tap template chips (Landlord questions, $50 grocery cart, buzzer codes).
5. **🏠 Interactive Housing Pipeline:** Track room leads, viewing times, and change statuses (`Inquiry Sent`, `Viewing Set`, `Top Choice`, `Lease Signed`) with 1-tap map zoom.
6. **💰 Real-Time Financial Engine:** Tracks liquid cash ($3,000 debit minus logged spending), monthly burn, and income surplus from remote AI tasking ($20–$40/hr USD).
7. **🥗 Chrono-Nutrition & Health Care OS:** Synchronized meal timing windows, whole-food grocery blueprint under $50/week, and toe infection recovery tracker.
8. **🧭 Hardware Directional Compass GPS:** Real-time mobile compass heading and glowing radar cone.
